Choose a model tier

You can change this later from the overlay's settings.

Locate your ER:LC window

Launch Roblox and join ER:LC first, then detect the window. You can fine-tune the region manually if detection misses.

Calibrate the speed readout

Drag the sliders so the box lines up over ER:LC's on-screen speed display (default bottom-right). The autopilot reads this via OCR since it has no other way to know your real speed.

Controls & safety

The autopilot drives using the same keys you would:

W A S DDrive (throttle / steer / brake)
PParking brake
QLeft turn signal
ERight turn signal
GHazards
HHorn
F9Kill switch — disengages instantly, from anywhere
Safety: This drives your keyboard for you. Keep a hand near it, watch the road, and expect it to make mistakes — it is not a replacement for your attention. Press F9 at any time to instantly disengage, or click Disengage in the overlay. Using automation in online games may violate that game's rules — that's on you to check.
